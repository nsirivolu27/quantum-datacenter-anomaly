Now imagine the whole hall doing that

Each server keeps its own listeners next to it. Records flow into a collector that only ever sees clean, time-stamped lines, so adding servers adds lines, not new kinds of work.

Rules are simple questions with numbers in them

6

Every finding lands in one of three bins

Broken, look closer, or someone did something they should not. The bins never merge, and each finding says what it cannot prove.

Broken

A cooling fault. Fix it fast. Nobody is attacking anything.

Look closer

Heavy work with no approved job, a steady heartbeat, a refused request. A person decides.

Should not have happened

Touching the decoy, probing ports, a changed model file. Still not proof of who or why.
